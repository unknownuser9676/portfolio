SUBJECT / ADESH

I find myself
drawn to what's possible.

I'm Adesh — a curious mind from Nairobi, interested in the space where technology, design and imagination meet.

I like understanding how things work, questioning how they could work differently, and turning ideas into something tangible. Sometimes that's a useful tool. Sometimes it's a visual experiment. Sometimes it's just a question I can't leave alone.

My interests don't fit neatly into one category, and I don't particularly want them to. I'm drawn to building, creative problem-solving, thoughtful design, music, film and the endless possibilities of technology.

Still learning. Still becoming. Still curious.
